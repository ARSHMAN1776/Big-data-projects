A permutation test shuffles results within each brand to check if the gap is real. p < 0.01 means very unlikely to be chance.

Key Data Findings

    Marketing Recommendations

    1
    Post Reels consistently

    Short-form video outperforms static images across Pakistani fashion brands on Instagram and TikTok.

    2
    Leverage Eid & seasonal peaks

    Engagement spikes during Eid-ul-Fitr, Eid-ul-Adha, and summer lawn season. Plan 2 weeks ahead.

    3
    Post 7–10 PM PKT

    Pakistani audiences are most active in the evening. Schedule posts for maximum reach.

    4
    Bilingual captions

    Mix Urdu/Roman Urdu with English to connect with both urban and regional audiences.

    Collection rules

    • Use public brand pages only — no scrapers or bots.
    • Take the 30 newest posts at least 7 days old — newer posts have had less time to accumulate engagement.
    • Facebook: "likes" = total reactions. TikTok: use plays for views.
    • Record followers at time of collection — this enables fair brand comparison.
