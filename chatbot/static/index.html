How to read this page
Typical post (median)
The middle post when all posts are lined up from worst to best. Half did better, half did worse. It is not thrown off by one viral post, which is why we use it instead of the average.
n = 30
The number of posts behind a bar. Bars with fewer than 5 posts are striped red because a few posts can swing them a lot.
Engagement per 100 followers
Likes plus comments (plus shares), divided by followers, times 100. It lets a small brand and a big brand be compared fairly. If likes or followers are missing, the page says which measure it used instead.
“Could easily be chance”
We shuffle the results among posts of the same brand and platform to see how often luck alone makes a gap this big. A small p means luck is unlikely. A gap that could be chance is a hint to test, not a finding.
Red marker
A caution worth reading before you trust the card.

Rules that keep the numbers comparable

  • Look at public brand pages only. Do not use scrapers, bots or fake accounts: they break the platforms' rules.
  • Take the 30 newest posts that are at least 7 days old, so new posts do not look weak. Skip pinned posts.
  • Facebook: “likes” means the total reactions number. TikTok: use the plays number for views.
  • Do not copy usernames or comment text. We only count comments.
